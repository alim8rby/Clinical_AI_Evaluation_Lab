## V4 hardening outcome

The major methodological limitations identified after V4.10 were addressed:

1. Production retrieval now supports semantic Ollama embeddings.
2. Semantic answer and grounding evaluation was added alongside deterministic baselines.
3. ClinicalQA-v1 expanded from 8 to 40 questions.
4. A separate 12-case retrieval challenge set was added.
5. Failure classification now consumes the stronger evaluation signals.

## Remaining limitations

- The controlled corpus is still compact and depression-only.
- The benchmark and challenge set are engineering datasets, not clinically validated datasets.
- Model-assisted evaluation is not clinical ground truth.
- Human calibration of semantic evaluation remains future work.
- Semantic embedding quality is tied to the selected local embedding model.
- No hosted cloud deployment, managed database, distributed tracing, or external monitoring is provisioned.
- No real patient data or autonomous clinical decision-making is supported.

## Freeze

V4.15 is complete and frozen. Further work belongs to V5 unless an explicit scope decision reopens hardening.
