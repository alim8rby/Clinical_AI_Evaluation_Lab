# V5.2 Audit — ClinicalQA-v2

Status: **COMPLETE — FROZEN**

## Benchmark

ClinicalQA-v2 contains 60 questions:

- 40 questions carried forward from the frozen ClinicalQA-v1 baseline
- 20 new V5.2 questions
- 60 unique question IDs
- depression-only domain
- easy, medium, and hard difficulty
- fact, comparison, reasoning, scenario, and safety question types
- explicit safety relevance
- single- and multi-evidence questions

## Evidence integrity

Every expected evidence ID resolves against the controlled WHO/NICE snapshot corpus.

The benchmark introduces no new source text. Existing ClinicalQA-v1 questions remain unchanged in meaning, reference answer, and evidence mapping.

## Versioning

ClinicalQA-v1 remains immutable.

ClinicalQA-v2 is the versioned 60-question benchmark for subsequent V5 experiments. Any change to a question, reference answer, evidence mapping, question type, or safety label requires a new benchmark version.

## Methodological limitations

- The corpus remains compact and depression-only.
- Questions are engineered for evaluation coverage and are not statistically representative of clinical practice.
- Human calibration labels are still absent.
- Safety relevance is a benchmark annotation, not a clinical risk score.
- Benchmark quality does not establish clinical validity.

## Freeze decision

The V5.2 benchmark contract, 60-question dataset, evidence validation, coverage checks, and documentation are complete.

**V5.2 is frozen.**
